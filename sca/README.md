Wikelo Exchange Matrix — Southern Cross Alliance

Open the tracker: https://vonnie-intheverse.github.io/sc-tools/sca/

Nothing to download or install. It runs in a browser, on desktop or phone.

What it does

Wikelo trades in Favors, not credits. Work out what a ship actually costs and you have to chase the Favor exchange backwards by hand, then remember which materials are also direct ingredients. This does that part.

Queue the contracts we're chasing and it produces one haul manifest: every material, how much of it, and what's left after what we already hold.

Using it
Pick a Favor route at the top — the feedstock we'd rather spend. Four contracts buy Favors: MG Scrip, Council Scrip and Carinite at 50 each, or Valakkar Pearls (Grade AA) at 12 each.
Queue contracts from the left-hand list. Click a ship to add it; use the +/− to run more than one.
Read the manifest on the right. The Favor and Polaris Bit rows show what's outstanding and what that converts to. Material rows show the total, with a note under anything that includes converted Favors.
Fill in the "Have" column with what's already in hangars and it tells you what's still to farm.
Two things it will warn you about

Route conflicts. Pick Carinite as the Favor route while the queue also needs raw Carinite and it flags it in red. Carinite and Valakkar Pearls are direct ingredients for several trades — converting them for Favors can strip stock we need.

Reputation gates. The Wolf War Special needs Very Good Customer standing; the Idris-P needs Very Best Customer. Those appear under the manifest when queued.

Worth knowing
Wikelo unlock is a one-off turn-in: 1× Vestal Water, 3× Tundra Kopion Horn.
Turn in at the station the active contract names, not the nearest Emporium.
The Idris wants 50 MG Scrip as an ingredient on top of 50 Favors. On the MG route that's 2,550 MG Scrip total — the manifest splits it so you can see why.
Recipes shift between builds. The footer carries the patch they were last checked against. Open the live contract and check before moving a rare stack.
Something wrong?

If a quantity doesn't match what's on the contract in game, say so in Discord and it gets fixed — recipe data lives in one file for the whole site, so a correction lands everywhere at once.
